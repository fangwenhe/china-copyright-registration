# Works Copyright (作品著作权) Registration Checklist — China

> Practical checklist for registering **creative works copyright** with the China Copyright Protection Center (CCPC): text, art/design, photography, music, video/audiobooks. For software use the [software copyright checklist](software-copyright-checklist.md) instead.

## Covered work types

| Type (中文) | Examples |
|---|---|
| 文字作品 (written works) | Books, articles, scripts, novels, course materials |
| 美术作品 (art works) | Logos, illustrations, packaging design, cartoon characters |
| 摄影作品 (photography) | Photos, product photography |
| 音乐作品 (music) | Songs, compositions, jingles (score + lyrics) |
| 视听作品 (audiovisual) | Short films, animations, video courses, audiobooks |
| 设计图/模型 (designs/models) | Engineering drawings, product design drawings |

## Documents to prepare

### 1. Application form
- CCPC online form: work title, type, creation date, first publication date (or "not published"), author(s), owner, creation method (independent/commissioned/cooperative)

### 2. Identity documents
- Company: business license / certificate of incorporation (+ Chinese translation for foreign entities)
- Individual: ID / passport (+ Chinese translation)
- Agency filing: power of attorney

### 3. Work samples (作品样本)
- Written works: full text or representative chapters (PDF)
- Art/photography: high-resolution images; for series, cover sheet + representative works
- Music: audio file + score/lyrics document
- Audiovisual: video file; long works may submit representative clips + full description
- File the **complete work** where practical; examiners may request the full version

### 4. Creative statement (创作说明)
- Short document describing: creation intent, process, originality points, reference materials
- For commissioned/cooperative works: contract or rights assignment document proving ownership chain

## Process & timeline

| Stage | Time |
|---|---|
| Online filing + upload | 1 day |
| Formality check | 1–2 weeks |
| Examination | 30–60 working days (varies by work type) |
| Certificate | electronic first, paper by mail |

- **Official fee**: category-dependent, generally ¥100–300/件 for most work types (confirm current CCPC price list; some categories/promotional periods are free)
- Agency fee (if used): separate, negotiable

## Foreign creators: special notes

1. China is a **Berne Convention** member — your work is protected automatically, but a Chinese certificate is the convenient evidence for local enforcement.
2. English documents generally need a **Chinese translation** for filing; the work itself (book, music, image) does not need translation.
3. For works first published abroad, provide first-publication evidence; Berne protects works of member-country nationals.
4. Register **before** posting full-resolution assets on Chinese platforms if takedown risk matters.

## After registration

- Protection term: **author's life + 50 years** (individual); **50 years from first publication** (legal entities)
- Use the certificate for: platform takedowns (Tmall/Taobao/Douyin/Xiaohongshu), licensing, publishing deals, litigation evidence
