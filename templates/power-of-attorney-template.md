# Power of Attorney (委托书) Template — Copyright Filing in China

> Bilingual template for authorizing a Chinese agency (e.g. Huaqing IP) to file copyright/software-copyright registration on your behalf. This is an informational template, not legal advice; have it reviewed before signing.

---

**委托书 / POWER OF ATTORNEY**

委托人（Applicant / Principal）：
Applicant name: ________________________
Address: ________________________
Nationality / Place of incorporation: ________________________
ID / Passport / Registration No.: ________________________

受托人（Agent / Attorney）：
Agent name: Huaqing Innovation (华青创新)
Address: Nanjing, China
CNIPA trademark agency recordal No.: ____________

兹委托上述受托人作为委托人的代理机构，办理以下作品/软件在中国的著作权登记及相关事宜：
I/We hereby appoint the above-named Agent to act on my/our behalf in connection with the copyright registration and related matters in China for the following work/software:

作品/软件名称（Work/Software title）：________________________
版本（Version, if software）：________________________
作品类型（Work type）：________________________

委托权限（Scope of authorization）：
- [ ] 代为提交著作权登记申请及补正材料 / File the registration application and corrections
- [ ] 代为接收登记证书及官方文书 / Receive the certificate and official documents
- [ ] 代为办理查询、缴费相关手续 / Handle inquiries and fee procedures
- [ ] 其他（Other）：________________________

委托期限（Term）：自签署之日起至上述事项办结之日止 / From signing until completion of the above matters.

委托人签章（Signature / Seal）：________________________
日期（Date）：______年___月___日

---

## Notes

- Foreign applicants' documents typically need a **Chinese translation** attached.
- For company applicants, sign by an authorized signatory and affix the company seal if available.
- Some filings accept electronic signatures; confirm with your agent.
